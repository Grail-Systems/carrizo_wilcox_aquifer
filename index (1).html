Grail Systems

Public records, put where people can see them.

Grail Systems builds tools that pull government data into one place, so a community can see who is deciding what, who is paying for it, and what it costs them.

Designed and built by Fawn Elizabeth Zion, systems architect, Lufkin, Texas.

Aquifer Defense Grid

Built for the East Texas Legacy Coalition

Data centers and power plants are lining up for water and power in Deep East Texas, above the Carrizo-Wilcox Aquifer that the region drinks from. The Grid puts every project, permit, water level and campaign donation on one public map, and every point links back to the record it came from.

Sources: ERCOT, EPA ECHO, the Texas Water Development Board, the Texas Ethics Commission, LegiScan and Texas Legislature Online, the Angelina County Appraisal District, and public information requests.

  • The map

    Projects, grid requests, aquifer water levels and EPA violations across 14 East Texas counties.

  • Wall of Shame

    Energy and utility money to the officials who represent Deep East Texas, next to how they voted.

  • Records requests

    Every request filed, what each agency did with it, and what is still being withheld.
