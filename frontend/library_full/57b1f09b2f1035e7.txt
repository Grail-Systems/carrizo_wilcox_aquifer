The Respondent owns and operates a wastewater treatment facility located at 3510 Southwood Drive, in Lufkin, Angelina County, Texas (the "Facility"). The Facility is near or adjacent to water in the state as defined in TEX. WATER CODE § 26.001(5). 2. During an investigation at the Facility conducted on August 29, 2023 through September 12, 2023, an investigator documented from August 26, 2023 through August 29, 2023 there were two unauthorized discharges of untreated sewage wastewater due to clogged/inoperable pumps and pump servicing at the Ellen Trout Lift Station which resulted in an estimated total of 6,100 gallons sewage wastewater discharged and fish kill. 3. The Executive Director recognizes that by August 31, 2023, the Respondent implemented the following corrective measures at the Facility: a. Ceased unauthorized discharges, unclogged, cleaned, and repaired both pumps at the lift station; b. Submitted noncompliance notification forms to the Beaumont Regional Office and a work order showing the lift station pumps are both operational; and City of Lufkin DOCKET NO. 2024-0641-MWD-E Page 2 c. Cleaned, removed sewage debris, and disinfected the area by flushing the unnamed tributary and Mill Creek with approximately 200,000 gallons of water from a Public Drinking Water ground storage tank situated adjacent to the discharge. As evidenced by Finding of Fact No. 2, the Respondent failed to prevent an unauthorized discharge of sewage into or adjacent to any water in the state, in violation of 30 TEX. ADMIN.
