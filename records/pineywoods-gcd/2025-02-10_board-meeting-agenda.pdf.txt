Agenda of Meeting of the  
Pineywoods Groundwater Conservation District 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Board of Directors of the Pineywoods Groundwater Conservation District will 
meet on Monday, the 10th day of February, 2025, at 12:00 p.m. at Commercial Bank of Texas, 215 Main St. 
Nacogdoches Texas, in accordance with the Texas Open Meeting Act, Chapter 551 of the Texas Government 
Code or (as amended) at which time the following items will be discussed and action may be taken: 
 
Public Hearing 12:00 p.m. - Receive comments regarding new permit applications for WattBridge Longleaf 
Generating Station, Angelina County and changes to PGCD rules and policies. 
Public Hearing 
1. Open public hearing; 
2. Receive public comments regarding new water well applications for WattBridge Longleaf Generating Station 
for two wells with total use of 80 mgy, south of Kurth Lake, in Angelina County.   
3. Receive public comments regarding recommended changes to PGCD’s rules and policies. 
4. Close public hearing. 
Written comments regarding the proposed permit applications must be received at the PGCD office, 202 E. Pilar 
St., Room 134, Nacogdoches, Texas or by email to staff@pgcd.org no later than 5:00 p.m., Friday February 7, 
2024.   
 
Board Meeting 
1. Call to Order and establish a quorum 
 
2. Public Comments 
 
3. Approval of the minutes of the meetings of November 18, 2024. 
 
4. Review and possible approve application for new permits for WattBridge Longleaf Generating Station 
Wells # 1 & # 2. 
 
5. Review and possible approve recommended changes to PGCD rules and policies. 
 
6. Presentation by Dr. Bill Hutchison, P.E., P.G.  
 
7. General Manager’s Financial Report 
a. Review and approval, if needed, of bills, banking and other financials 
b. Review and possibly approve 4th quarter 2024 investment report 
 
8. General Manager’s District Report 
 
9. Review annual static well level report 
 
10. Review and possible approval of Annual Management Report 
 
11. Announcement of the date and location of the next meeting  
 
 
12.  Adjourn. 
 
If during the course of the meeting, any discussion of any item on the agenda should be held in a closed 
meeting, the board will conduct a closed meeting in accordance with the Texas Open Meetings Act, Texas 
Gov’t Code, Chapter 551, Subchapters D and E. 
Dated this day the 28th day of January, 2025, A.D. 
 
By: John McFarland 
General Manager
