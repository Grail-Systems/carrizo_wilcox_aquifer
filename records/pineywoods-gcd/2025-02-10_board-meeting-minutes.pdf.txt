5 
Presentation by Dr. Bill Hutchison, P.E., P.G. 
a. Notes & Discussion:  Bill discussed the need for adjusting the rules to include a 
Hydrologist report at a lower level than currently required.  Recommended the 
report be required for all wells with 10” minimum casing size and 50 million 
gallons aggregate per year permitted volume or higher. 
 

6 
Review and possible approval of recommended changes to PGCD rules & policies: 
a. Discussion:  Motion to adjust rules as recommended by Dr. Hutchison 
 as well as to charge the well applicant for district cost to review the report for a 
sum not to exceed $1,000.  Included in motion to change 14g to change 
government code section to 2001 and add reference to Texas Water Code section 
36.418; 14h to “include conditions that would require installation of monitoring 
wells accessible to District staff, outside of area of influence of well application”.   
Also, part of motion, add the following; ”in order to comply with Senate Bill 1893 
and Government Code Chapter 620, PGCD prohibits the use of the social media 
service “TikTok” and any other successor application developed by ByteDance as 
well as all other software, applications, developers, hardware, equipment and 
manufacturers on the prohibited technologies list on District equipment” to the 
Networking Acceptable Use Policy. 
b. Motion to approve: 
Tommy Carswell 
Second:  Stephen Raley 
c. Motion carried?  Yes
