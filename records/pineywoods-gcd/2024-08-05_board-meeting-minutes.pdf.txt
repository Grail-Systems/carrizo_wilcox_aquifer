Presentation by Tim Karczewski of Skelton Slusher Barnhill Watkins & Wells law 
firm.  “How To Handle Exporting Water Out Of The District” 
a. Discussion:  Tim suggested we look at other Groundwater Districts in the area 
and their rules on how to handle exporting water from the District and update 
accordingly.  John to get proposed changes together by next business meeting so 
that we can update the rules by early 2025. 

 
5 
Review and possibly approve application of new permit for Angelina WSC, well 
#5, & increases to permitted amounts for wells 2, 3, & 4: 
a. Discussion:  Talked about the need for increase on the existing well permits since 
they are at 80% of the permitted amount.  Consensus was that this should not be a 
problem.  Also discussed impact on the nearest irrigation well to the new well 
site.  Roger said that should not be a problem based on factors mentioned in the 
report.  Motion to approve as requested. 
 
b. Motion: Kevin Gee 
 
 
Second:  Roger Russell
