For landowners in the Pineywoods Groundwater Conservation District, which covers Angelina and Nacogdoches counties.

What filing a petition takes


Under District Rule 16.1(b)(5), a petition must:


    
  
  • be made on the district's own Petition to Adopt or Modify Rules form;
    • 
  
  • include the proposed rule text and a written explanation of its purpose;
    • 
  
  • include proof that you have a real property interest in groundwater inside the district; and
    • 
  
  • give your full name, phone, physical and mailing address, and email.
    • 



The board must act within 90 days: deny it with an explanation, or start rulemaking. The rule also says the petitioner reimburses the district for the cost of giving notice of a rulemaking hearing. We'll walk through all of this with you before anything is filed.



Ask the district to notify you directly


Under District Rule 16.2(d), anyone with an interest can ask the district in writing for notice of a hearing. The request lasts one year. Email this to gm@pgcd.org from your own email, with your name at the bottom:


Mr. Blough,

Under District Rule 16.2(d) and Texas Water Code §36.404(d), I request written notice, by email to [your email], of any hearing, prehearing conference, preliminary hearing, or continued setting on the Pitman Family Farms well permit applications, and of any other permit hearing the District schedules.

Thank you,
[your name]
[county]





  
Know the contest deadline. Under District Rule 16.4(a), anyone who intends to contest a permit application must give the district written notice of that intent at least five calendar days before the hearing. If no one does, the hearing is canceled and the board considers the permit at its next regular meeting.
