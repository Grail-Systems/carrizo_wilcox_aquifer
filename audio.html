Feb. 9, 2026

Lufkin Planning and Zoning Commission · Public hearing on a special use permit for a hotel (Request #26-02) · Full meeting: 11 minutes

The data center was not on the agenda. The hotel was, and a new hotel means new demand on the City’s water. Our founder spoke during the public hearing on that item.

Here is how the City’s draft minutes of the meeting record what she said:

“Mrs. Fawn Zion spoke and came forward with concerns regarding water usage for the hotel and expressed concerns regarding city-wide water usage and the proposed data center to be located outside the city limits.” Minutes of the Feb. 9, 2026 meeting, as drafted in the City’s Feb. 23, 2026 agenda packet. The commission approved them on Feb. 23 with no changes noted.

Here is what was said.

Clip 2: the public comment

She cites the lake level, the water demand of large data centers, and the City’s water rights: “You own the water rights, which means you own the choice.” She asks the commission to halt all new high-use permits, including this hotel, until a full public water study is done.

5:41 to 7:28 of the full recording

Clip 3: after public comment closed

At the commission table, members say the data center is outside the city limits, that anything past the commission goes to City Council, and that her statement will be “recorded in the minutes.”
